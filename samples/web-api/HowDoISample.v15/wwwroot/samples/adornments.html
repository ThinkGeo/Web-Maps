Adornments

Adornment layers are drawn on the server as one picture the size of the map, laid over it and drawn again whenever the view settles. The states are classed by population in the style; the legend is a LegendAdornmentLayer with one item per class. The scale line and bar read the scale of what is shown. The compass is a MagneticDeclinationAdornmentLayer - true north, magnetic north and the declination between them for the middle of the map - pinned to a corner and turned with the map. The graticule is a feature layer drawn as tiles; the zoom and compass buttons are the map's own.

Turn the map with a right-drag: the compass turns with it, and the scale still reads the scale of the map, not of the box the turned map covers.
