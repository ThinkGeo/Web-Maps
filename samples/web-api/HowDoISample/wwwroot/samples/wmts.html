WMTS

A WMTS does not draw to order. Its tiles were cut in advance on a matrix set, a fixed ladder of scales, and every layer names the one it belongs to. The server reads that ladder out of the capabilities document and asks on the right rung, then serves what comes back at raster/wmts-…/.

What the service says about itself

This is the difference from a WMS that matters when you write the server. A WMS is asked for an extent and answers with a picture of it. A WMTS can only hand over tiles it already has, so whatever asks has to speak in its matrix set, at its levels, in its format. Zoom past a layer's last level and there is nothing left to serve.
