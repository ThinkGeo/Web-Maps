Measure in the right projection

Web Mercator is a projection for looking at, not for measuring in. It keeps angles and pays for it with scale: everything is stretched by 1/cos(latitude), so an area comes out too big by the square of that, and the further from the equator the worse it gets. Click a park, then drag it north: the same park, the same size on the ground, and the map follows it without changing zoom, so what you see it swell to on the screen is the error in the table.

The geodesic figure is the answer on the ellipsoid itself, which is what a surveyor means. An equal-area projection matches it for area anywhere. A local grid - the state plane zone the county records are in - matches for both, inside the zone it was made for. Web Mercator matches neither, and only looks close near the equator.
